📦 SHAREBOX FOR GORDON COLLEGE
OPEN TO GC STUDENTS AND PERSONNEL ONLY

Donating is now easier for all Gordon College students and personnel using ShareBox.

Please sign in with your GC domain account to post donations.

What you need to do:

  • Choose an item you no longer use.
  • Post it with a photo, description, and condition.
  • Hand it over on campus at the pickup spot you chose.

How it works

  1. Post. Donors list an item with a photo, description, and condition.
  2. Browse. People in need look through the donations and check the condition.
  3. Contact. They check the pickup spot and contact the donor.
  4. Pass it on. Both sides arrange a pickup or hand-off.
